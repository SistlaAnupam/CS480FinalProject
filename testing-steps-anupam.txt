CS480 Hotel Management Application - Testing Steps
====================================================
Run clear.sql then seed.sql before starting.


----------------------------------------------------
MANAGER TESTS
----------------------------------------------------

Step 1 - Manager login (Alice)
Input SSN: 111111111
Expected: welcome message with name "Alice Johnson"

Step 2 - Manager login failure
Input SSN: 999999999
Expected: error message saying no manager found

Step 3 - Add a hotel
Name: "Miami Beach Resort"
Address: Ocean Dr, 100, Miami
Expected: success confirmation

Step 4 - Add a room to that hotel
Hotel: Miami Beach Resort, Room: 101
Windows: 3, Renovation year: 2023, Access: elevator
Expected: success

Step 5 - Update a hotel
Change name of Miami Beach Resort to "Miami Grand Resort"
Expected: success

Step 6 - Remove a room
Remove room 101 from Miami Grand Resort
Expected: success, room no longer appears in hotel listings

Step 7 - Top-k clients (k=2)
Expected output (ordered by bookings):
    Jane Smith  jane@test.com  - 2 bookings
    John Doe    john@test.com  - 1 booking
    (Tom also has 1 booking, John and Tom may appear in either order)

Step 8 - All rooms with booking counts
Expected: every room listed, most showing 0 bookings except:
    Hotel 1, Room 101 - 1 booking
    Hotel 1, Room 102 - 1 booking
    Hotel 3, Room 301 - 1 booking
    Hotel 3, Room 302 - 1 booking

Step 9 - Hotel stats
Expected:
    Grand Chicago Hotel  - 2 bookings, avg rating: NULL
    NYC Plaza            - 0 bookings, avg rating: NULL
    Windy City Hostel    - 2 bookings, avg rating: 0.5
    Miami Grand Resort   - 0 bookings, avg rating: NULL

Step 10 - City pair query
C1: Boston, C2: Chicago
Expected: Jane Smith (jane@test.com)
    - Jane has a Boston address and booked Hotel 1 (Chicago)
    - John should NOT appear (Chicago address, not Boston)
    - Tom should NOT appear (no Chicago booking)

Step 11 - Problematic Chicago hotels
Expected: Windy City Hostel
    - Located in Chicago
    - Avg rating 0.5 (< 2)
    - Booked by Jane (Boston) and Tom (Seattle), neither has a Chicago address
    - Grand Chicago Hotel should NOT appear (no reviews, no qualifying pattern)

Step 12 - Client spending report
Expected:
    John Doe    - $750.00   (5 nights * $150)
    Jane Smith  - $760.00   (4 nights * $150 + 2 nights * $80)
    Tom Green   - $160.00   (2 nights * $80)

Step 13 - Remove a client
First register a throwaway client through the application (see Step 16),
then come back and remove them here.
Expected: client no longer appears in any listing


----------------------------------------------------
CLIENT TESTS
----------------------------------------------------

Step 14 - Client login (John)
Input email: john@test.com
Expected: welcome message with name "John Doe"

Step 15 - Client login failure
Input email: nobody@test.com
Expected: error saying no client found

Step 16 - Register new client (Carol)
Name: Carol White
Email: carol@test.com
Address: Park Ave, 200, New York
Credit card: 4999999999999999, billing at same address
Expected: success
(This client is also used for Step 13)

Step 17 - Update client info
Login as John. Change name to "Jonathan Doe"
Expected: name updates successfully
Then attempt to change email - application should block this

Step 18 - Search available rooms
Input: 2026-06-01 to 2026-06-05 (no conflicts)
Expected: all rooms listed as available across all hotels

Then try: 2026-01-10 to 2026-01-13
Expected: Room 101 at Hotel 1 should NOT appear (overlaps John's existing booking)

Step 19 - Book a specific room
Login as John. Book Hotel 2, Room 201 from 2026-06-01 to 2026-06-05
Expected: success, new booking appears in Step 21

Step 20 - Book an already-taken room
Try to book Hotel 2, Room 201 for 2026-06-03 to 2026-06-07
(overlaps with booking from Step 19)
Expected: error saying the room is unavailable for those dates

Step 21 - View bookings
Login as John.
Expected: two bookings appear:
    Hotel 1, Room 101 (January)
    Hotel 2, Room 201 (June)
    Both showing hotel names and costs

Step 22 - Auto-book
Login as John. Hotel: Hotel 1, Dates: 2026-07-01 to 2026-07-03
Expected: system picks an available room (102 or 103) and confirms
booking with room number, hotel name, and dates

Step 23 - Auto-book with fallback
Hotel: Miami Grand Resort, Dates: 2026-08-01 to 2026-08-03
(room was deleted in Step 6, so no rooms available)
Expected: message saying no room is available at that hotel,
followed by a list of alternative hotels with availability in that range

Step 24 - Submit a review (valid)
Login as John. Review Hotel 1 (he has a booking there)
Rating: 8, Message: "Great stay, loved the location."
Expected: success

Step 25 - Submit a review (invalid)
Login as John. Attempt to review Hotel 3 (no booking there)
Expected: review is rejected

Step 26 - Re-run problematic hotels query (manager)
After Step 24 adds John's rating of 8 to Hotel 1, re-run the query.
Expected: Hotel 1 still does NOT appear
    (John has a Chicago address so he does not count toward the condition)
    Windy City Hostel should still appear unchanged
